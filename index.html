What it does

The six things it does every week.

01

Brief me before the meeting

Ask Sailor about anyone, and it tells you everything the thread knows. What you promised. What they said they wanted. Their kid's name.

No CRM has this, because nobody types it into a CRM. It only ever existed in the text.

02

Catch the ones waiting

Three people asked you something and never got an answer. Sailor finds them, shows you the actual message, and has a reply ready.

Including the promises. "I'll send those comps tonight" — four days ago.

03

Write it like I would

Sailor has read every message you ever sent this person — your greeting, your nickname for them, how you sign off. The draft comes back sounding like you on a good day.

Sailor never sends. It writes, you read it, you press send. Permanent rule, not a setting.

Security

Your messages never leave your Mac.

This isn't a privacy policy. It's the architecture — the data physically cannot move.

Computed on-device.

Every metric is plain SQL on your own machine. Only summaries ever leave, and only when you ask.

Work only.

Sailor sorts threads into work and personal, and reads only the work side. You see the full list and can change any of it in one tap.

It never sends.

Sailor drafts into your compose field. A human presses send, every time. No bulk messaging, no drip campaigns, no TCPA exposure.

Delete means delete.

One click removes everything, derived data included. Never sold, never trained on, and that's in writing.
