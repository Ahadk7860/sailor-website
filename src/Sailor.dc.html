Brief me before the meeting

Ask Sailor about anyone, and it tells you everything the thread knows. What you promised. What they said they wanted. Their kid's name.

No CRM has this, because nobody types it into a CRM. It only ever existed in the text.

Catch the ones waiting

Three people asked you something and never got an answer. Sailor finds them, shows you the actual message, and has a reply ready.

Write it like I would

Sailor has read every message you ever sent this person — your greeting, your nickname for them, how you sign off. The draft comes back sounding like you on a good day.
